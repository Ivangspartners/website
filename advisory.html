Three strategic priorities

Where we help leaders decide and act.

01

B2Cization

Rethink the role of intermediaries, strengthen customer relationships, and decide where direct engagement creates genuine competitive advantage.

  • Which parts of the customer relationship are we renting from someone else, and what does that cost us in margin and in knowledge?
  • Where does going direct create real advantage, and where does it only add cost and conflict with the partners we depend on?
  • What has to be true operationally — data, logistics, service, pricing — before direct engagement pays for itself?

02

AI and the business

Understand how AI reshapes value creation, competitive dynamics, and business models — and identify where to act.

  • Which parts of our value chain does AI make cheaper, and which parts does it make strategically irrelevant?
  • Where does it strengthen our position, and where does it lower the barrier for everyone else to do what we do?
  • What do we build, what do we buy, and what do we deliberately decide not to automate?

03

Superior customer experience

Turn customer experience from a functional responsibility into a source of differentiation and sustainable business performance.

  • What do customers actually value, as opposed to what our internal metrics reward us for improving?
  • Which parts of the experience would competitors find genuinely hard to copy, and are we investing there?
  • How does experience connect to the numbers the board reads — retention, pricing power, cost to serve?

How an engagement works

Four stages, agreed before we start.

01

Frame

A first conversation and a short written brief of the decision at stake, agreed with you before any work begins.

02

Diagnose

Evidence from your own business: numbers, customers, competitors, and candid conversations inside the organisation.

03

Choose

04

Embed

We stay through implementation until the owner on your side can carry it without us.
