Why it exists

What changed my career was exposure.

I used to believe careers grew with talent, effort and credentials. What changed mine was exposure: seeing how senior leaders weigh trade-offs, debate under constraint and decide with incomplete data.

Three times a CEO, a PhD, a teaching post at UCL and two decades advising executive teams at global brands taught me one thing. What those rooms share is not an industry. It is altitude. The Next Leap opens those rooms to people ready to enter them.

Giuseppe StiglianoCreator and curator of The Next Leap

FAQ

Questions from business leaders.

What is The Next Leap for individuals?

An executive learning and networking community, created and curated by Giuseppe Stigliano. It combines 22 on-demand strategic sessions, monthly live Q&A, CEO fireside chats and a moderated community of high-potential peers.

How is it different from a course or coaching?

Courses teach what to do, and coaching often focuses on how you feel. The Next Leap focuses on how top leaders think and decide, through direct exposure to them and to a curated peer group.

How much time does it take?

About 30 to 60 minutes a week on demand, plus one or two hours a month for optional live sessions. Everything is recorded.

Will I have direct access to Giuseppe?

Yes, through the monthly live Q&A and the community. Pro and Inner Circle members get additional access, and Inner Circle includes a private two-hour mentoring session.

How large is each cohort?

Foundation admits up to 100 members, Pro up to 50 and Inner Circle up to 10.

Is it global, and in which language?

Yes. Members join from many countries and industries. The programme runs in English, with subtitles, and sessions are scheduled across time zones where possible.

Is it relevant if I am changing role?

Yes. Many members use it to sound more credible in senior conversations and to position themselves as strategic contributors.
